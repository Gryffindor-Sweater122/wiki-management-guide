# Wiki Management Guide
A comprehensive, opinionated framework for building, organizing, and maintaining high‑quality wikis across any platform.

# Introduction
Welcome to the **Wiki Management Guide**, a structured resource designed to help you plan, build, and sustain a scalable wiki. Whether you're running a community wiki, a corporate knowledge base, or a personal documentation hub, this guide provides the architecture, workflows, and governance needed to keep your wiki healthy long‑term.

# What This Guide Covers
* **Content Architecture** — How to structure pages, categories, templates, and navigation

* **Editorial Workflow** — Roles, permissions, review processes, and version control

* **Style Guidelines** — Writing standards, formatting rules, and media usage

* **Maintenance & Quality Control** — Audits, cleanup routines, link checking, and lifecycle management

* **Automation & Tools** — Bots, scripts, extensions, and integrations

* **Community & Governance** — Policies, moderation, contributor onboarding
